\section{Proposed Architectures}
% ==========================================

\begin{frame}{Error Amplifier Topologies}
    To achieve the desired gain and PSRR without an external capacitor, three OTA topologies were evaluated:
    
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Standard 5T OTA:} Baseline for area and current consumption.
        \item \textbf{Telescopic OTA:} High gain and bandwidth, but limited output swing.
        \item \textbf{Current Mirror OTA:} Improved slew rate and decoupling of bandwidth from the input pair.
    \end{itemize}

% ==========================================
\section{Conclusion}
% ==========================================

\begin{frame}{Conclusion}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item The \textbf{Telescopic OTA} provided the best overall noise performance.
        \item The \textbf{Current Mirror OTA} offered superior dynamic response at the cost of slight area increase.
        \item All topologies successfully met the capacitor-less stability criteria across the specified load range.
    \end{itemize}
